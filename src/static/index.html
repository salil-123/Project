Alpha Earth base map, with greenery split into crops / trees / shrubs.

Server-side 10 m classification served as Earth Engine map tiles — crisp at any zoom, calibrated to India's prior, no downloads.

Base classes and the inference year live in the Model Zoo — open it to pick a starting scheme or change the Alpha Earth year.

🔗 Merge classes

      
Tick two or more leaf classes in the Hierarchy above (even from
        different splits/models), name them, and merge. It's a relabelling layer applied after
        inference — no retraining. Active merges show in the tree with a ✕ to undo.
