\noindent Year 2024 for both embeddings (the only year with usable Tessera coverage
over India). Classes: greenery, water, built-up, barren. Ground truth combined to
2{,}416 polygons; the dual-embedding master is 67{,}900 pixels $\times$ 196 columns.

\section{Models}
\textbf{Realistic (default):} one \texttt{LinearSVC} on Alpha Earth, trained on
polygon pixels pooled with WorldCover points ($w_{\text{wc}}{=}2$ for India's real
class prior). Server-side, no Tessera download.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{Detailed:} soft-vote of a calibrated \texttt{LinearSVC} on the
prior-aware AE pool (weight 0.7) with a calibrated \texttt{LinearSVC} on Tessera
(weight 0.3). Downloads an area's Tessera tiles on demand.
