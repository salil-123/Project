\begin{frame}{Task overview}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Build a Land-Use/Land-Cover classifier for India \textbf{from pre-trained
  satellite embeddings}, not hand-engineered spectral bands.
  \item Classes: \textbf{greenery, water, built-up, barren}.
  \item Attach \emph{both} Alpha Earth (64-d) and Tessera (128-d) embeddings per
  pixel, train a simple classifier, evaluate it \emph{honestly}.
  \item Serve it in an interactive tool where predictions can be inspected,
  corrected, and extended with new classes --- the seed of an evolving dataset.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Data sources}
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l l l}
\toprule
\textbf{Source} & \textbf{Role} & \textbf{Used by} \\
\midrule
IndiaSAT          & class labels (4-class)     & both models \\
FarmForest GT     & class labels (greenery)    & both models \\
GT\_BINARY        & class labels (water)       & both models \\
Alpha Earth       & 64-d features              & both models \\
Tessera           & 128-d features             & Detailed model \\
ESA WorldCover v200 & extra labels + class prior & Realistic + eval \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{1em}
\begin{itemize}\small
  \item Ground truth: \textbf{2{,}416 polygons} (heavily built-up: 58\%).
  \item Year 2024 for both embeddings (only year with Tessera coverage over India).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Building the training dataframe}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Diverse tile selection:} weighted set-cover over (1$^\circ$ block
  $\times$ class) $\Rightarrow$ 200 tiles, 679 polygons, 161 of India's 207 blocks.
  (Fixes geographic clustering, the main cause of over-optimistic scores.)
  \item \textbf{Download} the Tessera tiles for those areas ($\approx$24\,GB).
  \item \textbf{Sample} up to 100 random interior pixels per polygon.
  \item \textbf{Extract} Alpha Earth (server-side in GEE) + Tessera at the same
  points $\Rightarrow$ clean join.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{0.5em}
Result: \textbf{67{,}900 pixels $\times$ 196 columns}
(\texttt{polygon\_id, class, lat, lon, ae\_000..063, te\_000..127}).
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Two models}
\textbf{Realistic (default)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item One LinearSVC on \textbf{Alpha Earth}, trained on polygons \emph{pooled with
  WorldCover} ($w_{\text{wc}}{=}2$) for India's real class prior.
  \item Server-side, \textbf{no Tessera, no downloads}.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Detailed}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Soft-vote}: prior-aware AE model (0.7) $+$ Tessera model (0.3),
  averaging their class probabilities.
  \item Leans into rare-class detail; downloads an area's Tessera tiles on demand.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{How we measure: region-held-out}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Hold out \textbf{whole 1$^\circ$ map blocks} (30\%); train on the rest; test
  only on the unseen blocks $\Rightarrow$ no spatial cheating.
  \item Average over several random splits (\emph{seeds}) so the number isn't a
  fluke.
  \item \textbf{Macro-F1}: average F1 across all 4 classes equally --- so rare
  classes count as much as 92\%-dominant greenery (accuracy alone hides them).
  \item Also a \textbf{random-location} check vs ESA WorldCover (real $\approx$92\%
  greenery prior).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Why LinearSVC? (region-held-out, Alpha Earth, 5 seeds)}
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l c c l}
\toprule
\textbf{classifier} & \textbf{acc} & \textbf{macro-F1} & \textbf{kind} \\
\midrule
HistGradientBoosting & 0.869 & 0.869 & tree \\
\textbf{LinearSVC}   & \textbf{0.865} & \textbf{0.865} & linear \\
LDA                  & 0.865 & 0.864 & linear \\
RandomForest         & 0.863 & 0.861 & tree \\
kNN ($k{=}15$)       & 0.857 & 0.855 & instance \\
LogReg               & 0.855 & 0.855 & linear \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.8em}
\flushleft
\begin{itemize}\small
  \item All six cluster within $\approx$0.014 macro-F1 --- \textbf{the embedding
  carries the signal; the classifier barely matters.}
  \item So we pick the simplest strong model: \textbf{LinearSVC} is tied for best,
  yet tiny, fast, easy to \emph{retrain on the fly}, and \textbf{calibrates cleanly}
  --- which the Detailed mode's soft-vote needs.
  \item Both models use it: Realistic $=$ one LinearSVC; Detailed $=$ two calibrated
  LinearSVC, soft-voted.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\vspace{1em}
\begin{itemize}\small
  \item As asked, we compare AE, Tessera, and AE+Tessera together. Alpha Earth alone
  is already best/tied-best, and \textbf{naively concatenating AE+Tessera adds
  nothing over AE} --- combining them \emph{well} (soft-vote, next slide) is what
  pays off.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Result 2 --- where it fails, and a fix}
The one weak class is \textbf{greenery}, leaking mainly to \textbf{water}:
\vspace{0.4em}
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l c c c c}
\toprule
\textbf{true greenery $\rightarrow$} & greenery & water & barren & built-up \\
\midrule
Alpha Earth & 0.48 & \textbf{0.34} & 0.16 & 0.02 \\
Tessera     & \textbf{0.59} & 0.21 & 0.17 & 0.03 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.8em}
\flushleft
\begin{itemize}\small
  \item Tessera cuts the greenery$\rightarrow$water leak --- but costs built-up.
  \item \textbf{Soft-voting} AE + Tessera (vs naive concat) recovers both:
  macro-F1 \textbf{0.797}; calibrated version reaches \textbf{0.854 acc / 0.808 F1}.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Result 3 --- the reality check (random India)}
On 2{,}392 random points ($\approx$89\% greenery, like real India):
\vspace{0.4em}
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{l c c}
\toprule
\textbf{model} & \textbf{accuracy} & \textbf{greenery recall} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Realistic (AE + WorldCover)} & \textbf{0.828} & 0.83 \\
balanced soft-vote (AE+TE, no prior) & 0.425 & 0.38 \\
reconciled AE$_{0.7}$ $\oplus$ Tessera (Detailed) & 0.765 & 0.75 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.8em}
\flushleft
\begin{itemize}\small
  \item The balanced soft-vote \textbf{collapses to 0.43} on real locations --- it
  ignores the prior and over-calls rare classes.
  \item \textbf{The class prior is the biggest lever}; AE + WorldCover wins overall.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Key takeaways}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A foundation embedding $+$ a \textbf{linear} model reaches $\approx$83\% on
  random India --- no spectral engineering, at 10\,m.
  \item \textbf{Data diversity}, not the embedding source, drives generalization.
  \item \textbf{Tessera does not earn its place} under India's real prior; it only
  helps on artificially class-balanced tests (hence: secondary "Detailed" mode).
  \item \textbf{The class prior dominates}: 0.43 $\rightarrow$ 0.83 by pooling
  WorldCover.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{The interactive tool}
\begin{itemize}
  \item FastAPI backend $+$ Leaflet map; pick an area $+$ mode, get a colored
  per-cell class overlay.
  \item Two modes: \textbf{Realistic} (instant, anywhere) / \textbf{Detailed}
  (downloads Tessera on demand).
  \item Detailed mode fetches each tile via \textbf{chunked-parallel byte-range
  download} ($\approx$2$\times$ faster than a single stream --- the server throttles
  per-connection, not per-IP).
  \item Draw polygons/points, add \textbf{new classes} (orchard, tea), persisted ---
  the seed of a user-driven, evolving dataset.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.6em}
\textbf{Honest limits:} numbers are agreement vs WorldCover / own polygons, not
field truth; 4 static classes only; greenery$\leftrightarrow$water is the residual
hard case (NDVI / temporal = next step).
\end{frame}
